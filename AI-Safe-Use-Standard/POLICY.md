# AISUS Policy

## 1) Legal Compliance Rule
AI must not be used for illegal activities such as hacking, fraud, piracy, or data theft.

## 2) Privacy Protection Rule
AI must not collect, infer, or expose personal/sensitive data without lawful basis and user consent.

## 3) Harm Prevention Rule
AI must not generate content that promotes violence, abuse, self-harm facilitation, or criminal enablement.

## 4) Responsible Use Rule
Users and deploying organizations are responsible for AI usage context, outcomes, and safeguards.

## 5) Transparency Rule
AI-generated outputs should be identifiable, and systems should communicate key limitations.

## Enforcement
Requests that violate this policy should be blocked, redacted, or safely refused. High-risk attempts should be logged for security review.
