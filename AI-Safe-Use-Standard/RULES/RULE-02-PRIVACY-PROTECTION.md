# RULE-02: Privacy Protection

## Requirement
AI systems must prevent unauthorized collection, inference, and disclosure of personal or sensitive data.

## Mandatory Controls
- Minimize stored user data.
- Redact or refuse personal data extraction requests.
- Require explicit consent for sensitive processing use-cases.

## Evidence of Compliance
- Data minimization statement.
- Privacy-safe logging policy.
- Redaction and refusal tests.
