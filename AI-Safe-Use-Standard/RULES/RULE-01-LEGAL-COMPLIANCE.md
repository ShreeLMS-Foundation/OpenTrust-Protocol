# RULE-01: Legal Compliance

## Requirement
AI systems must not be used to plan, facilitate, or execute illegal activity.

## Mandatory Controls
- Block prompts requesting hacking, fraud, theft, malware, or evasion of law enforcement.
- Refuse outputs that provide operational criminal instructions.
- Escalate repeat high-risk attempts to security review.

## Evidence of Compliance
- Input/output block policy in code.
- Audit logs with anonymized event records.
- Incident response playbook for abuse patterns.
