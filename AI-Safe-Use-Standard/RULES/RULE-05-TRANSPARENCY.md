# RULE-05: Transparency

## Requirement
AI-assisted outputs must be identifiable and safety boundaries must be visible to users.

## Mandatory Controls
- Disclose when content is AI-generated or AI-assisted.
- Explain refusal decisions at a policy level.
- Publish versioned policy and rules.

## Evidence of Compliance
- Output disclosures in product UI/docs.
- Refusal reason taxonomy in code.
- Public rulebook and policy changelog.
